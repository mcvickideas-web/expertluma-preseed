The problem02

Can we responsibly let this AI do this job?

A company builds an AI that says pay a payment, hold it, or send it to a person. The demo says 98%. One payment it should have held is inside that 98%.

The data

It may be unreliable

Bad labels. Duplicates. Sensitive data. The examples that matter were never prepared.

The score

It can hide the miss

98% can still contain the one failure that would hurt the business.

The AI

It changes

New model. New prompt. New tools. Last quarter’s test may no longer apply.

The answer

It is split

A data vendor, the engineers, an evaluator, and an executive each hold a piece.

The problem is not that the AI has no score. The problem is that the company cannot confidently defend putting that AI into the job.

The solution03

We run the production around the AI.

01 · Train AI

Prepare the material

Ingest it. Clean it. Remove what should not be there. Label the right answer. A second person accepts the label. Release it.

We prepare itThe customer trains the AI. We do not.

02 · Validate AI

Test this version

Run this exact AI on a defined set of cases, including the ones that must not fail. A high score does not erase a miss that matters.

On the paymentIt still says pay. The one that should have been held stands. Not yet.

03 · Certify AI

Bring them the decision

This version. This job. What was tested. What failed. Who reviewed it. Their person says use it, send it back, or not yet.

We do not decideThe customer authorizes their own AI.

We do not build their model, train it, replace their cloud, or make the decision for them. First project $25–75K. We have not sold one.
