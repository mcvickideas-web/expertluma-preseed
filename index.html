Problem02

A high score is how the AI gets shipped.

Companies already have a model, an agent, or a vendor product. The average looks fine, so the meeting says go live. The miss that matters never owns the decision.

01 · The work

Judgment

Someone must review the cases that must not fail. That work sits in a labeling tool, a spreadsheet, and an inbox.

We produce the judgment and accept it. Submitting is not accepting.

02 · The test

This version

Scores average the miss away. A rename is treated as an improvement. Most cases passed, so the failure never stops the program.

We measure this version for this use. A required miss can block while the score stays high.

03 · The permission

A name

Evidence, evaluation, and approval live apart. “Looks good enough” has no owner. If it fails in the world, a person still owns it.

A named person at their company decides. Use it, send it back, or not ready.
