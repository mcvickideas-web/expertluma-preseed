The problem02

Someone has to own the failure.

A company built an AI to handle payments: pay it, hold it, or send it to a person. The demo says 98%. The one payment that should have been held is inside that 98%.

What they have

Three piles

A labeling vendor’s spreadsheet. Last quarter’s accuracy slide. A meeting where someone is asked to say yes.

What they lack

One file

This version. The cases that matter. Who labeled them. Who accepted the labels. Who said yes.

Who feels it

The executive

The person who would have to explain a wrong payment. They will not put their name on a slideshow.

So the AI waits. Or it ships, and a failure has no owner. The same stall hits a drafted clinical note, a customer refund, or a public-service decision. The buyer is that executive, not the team that built the model.
