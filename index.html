02

A labeling vendor's spreadsheet. A benchmark slide. A meeting where someone is asked to say yes.

WHAT THEY LACK

This version. The cases that matter. What changed. Who reviewed it. What evidence supports the decision.

WHO FEELS IT

The executive

The person who eventually has to answer: why did we allow this AI to do this job?

The problem is not that AI has no score. The problem is that the company cannot confidently defend putting it into the job.

THE SOLUTION

03

We run the production around the AI.

The customer brings the AI. Expertluma handles the controlled work required to produce reliable inputs, measure behavior, assemble evidence, and support the authorization decision.

01 · TRAIN AI

Prepare the material

Ingest data. Clean it. Detect sensitive data. Label examples. Review and accept the work. Preserve provenance. Release accepted training/evaluation material.

02 · VALIDATE AI

Prove the behavior

Test the exact AI version against a defined examination. Measure outcomes. Surface critical failures. Separate measurement from human review. Compare versions.

03 · CERTIFY AI

Support the decision

Bind evidence to this AI version and this job. Check policy, review, identity and conditions. The customer decides: authorized, not authorized, or not yet.

We do not build or train the customer's model. We operate the production around it.
